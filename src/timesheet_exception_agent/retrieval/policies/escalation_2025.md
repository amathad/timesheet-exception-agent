# Escalation to the Next-Level Manager (2025 Edition, Superseded)

States: ALL
Effective: 2025-01-01
Until: 2026-01-01

## Manager who replies but has not decided by 9:30 AM
Under the 2025 rules, a manager who has replied but not approved or corrected the time is escalated to the next-level manager at 9:30 AM.

## Next-level manager response time
Under the 2025 rules, the next-level manager is expected to reply within one hour. Send them a reminder after 30 minutes, then pass the exception to the coordinator.
