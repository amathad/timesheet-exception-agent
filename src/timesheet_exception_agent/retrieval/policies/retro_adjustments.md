# Retro Adjustments

States: ALL
Effective: 2026-01-01

## What a retro adjustment is
A retro adjustment corrects hours or pay codes for a pay period that has already been processed. It is paid or recovered on a later paycheck rather than the current one.

## Corrections after the lock
Once timesheets lock at 10:00 AM Monday, a manager cannot change time themselves. A correction the manager asks for on Monday after the lock can still be entered by the coordinator before end of day. A correction that arrives Tuesday or later becomes a retro adjustment.

## Recording a retro adjustment
Record the original hours, the corrected hours, the reason, who asked for the change, and when. Retro adjustments also show how far a history-based or standard-hours estimate was off, which feeds back into future estimates.
