# Escalation to the Next-Level Manager (Utah Locations)

States: UT
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Manager on leave or out of office
For Utah locations, a manager shown as out of office is escalated only after the site HR partner confirms the leave in the HR system. Until HR confirms, send the manager a reminder instead of escalating.

## Next-level manager response time
For Utah locations, the next-level manager is given 60 minutes to reply, because Utah sites run a later morning shift change. If they do not reply within 60 minutes, the exception passes to the coordinator.
