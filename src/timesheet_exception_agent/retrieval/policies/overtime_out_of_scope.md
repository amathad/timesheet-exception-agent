# State Wage and Overtime Rules

States: ALL
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Scope
State-specific wage and overtime rules, such as daily overtime thresholds, meal and rest break premiums, and minimum wage changes, are handled by the payroll compliance team. They are out of scope for exception outreach.

## Referring questions
If a manager raises an overtime or wage-law question while resolving an exception, note it for the coordinator and refer the question to the compliance team. Do not calculate overtime premiums as part of resolving an exception.
