# Lock-Day Timeline

States: ALL
Effective: 2026-01-01

## When the exception report is ready
The exception report for a pay period is ready before Monday of lock week. It lists every employee whose time is unsubmitted, unapproved, or flagged for unusual hours, along with the employee's manager. Each payroll coordinator works the exceptions for the states assigned to them.

## Monday morning before the lock
Coordinators start working exceptions at 8:00 AM Mountain time on Monday. Between 9:00 and 10:00 AM managers have their final correction window: this is the last hour in which a manager can approve or change a timesheet themselves.

## The 10:00 AM timesheet lock
At 10:00 AM Mountain time on Monday, timesheets lock. After the lock, managers can no longer approve or change time. Any exception still waiting on a manager at the lock passes to the coordinator.

## Monday after the lock
From the lock until end of day Monday, only the coordinator can change timesheets. The coordinator uses this time for history-based approvals, teammate confirmations, and standard hours.

## Tuesday payroll processing
Payroll is processed on Tuesday. No changes of any kind are allowed while processing runs, including by coordinators.

## Wednesday onward
Timesheets unlock on Wednesday. Anything fixed from Wednesday on is a retro adjustment that appears on a later paycheck, not the current one.
