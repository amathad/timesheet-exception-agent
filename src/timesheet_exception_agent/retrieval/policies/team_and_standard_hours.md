# Teammate Confirmation and Standard Hours

## Asking a teammate
When history cannot be used, the coordinator may ask someone on the employee's team, usually a teammate under the same manager, whether the employee worked their regular schedule. Ask about the employee's recent hours only; a teammate cannot approve time. The coordinator has until end of day Monday for this.

## Standard hours by job type
If no teammate can confirm the hours by end of day Monday, the coordinator enters standard hours for the job type: full-time hourly employees get 8 hours a day, which is 80 hours for a two-week period; part-time hourly employees get their scheduled hours on file, or 4 hours a day if no schedule is on file. New hires are prorated from their start date.

## Correcting standard hours later
Standard hours are a placeholder so the employee is paid on time. Once the manager confirms the actual hours, any difference is corrected through a retro adjustment on a later paycheck.

## Managers with no next-level manager
When the manager is unavailable and has no next-level manager, there is nobody else to approve. Contact one teammate about the employee's recent hours and pass the exception to the coordinator, who applies history or standard hours.
