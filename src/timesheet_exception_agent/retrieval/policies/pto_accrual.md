# Paid Time Off Accrual

States: ALL
Effective: 2026-01-01

## How PTO accrues
Full-time employees accrue paid time off each pay period based on years of service. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to hours worked. Accrual balances appear on each pay statement.

## Requesting PTO
Employees request PTO through the time-off system at least two weeks ahead when possible. Managers approve or deny requests in the same system. Approved PTO appears on the timesheet under the PTO pay code.

## Carryover and payout
Unused PTO above the annual carryover limit is forfeited at year end unless state law requires payout. Questions about balances go to the benefits team, not the payroll coordinators.
